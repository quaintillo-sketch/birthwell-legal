Privacy Policy

BirthWell · Effective October 27, 2026

BirthWell is made by QuaintInc LLC (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information BirthWell uses and why. The short version: you don’t need an account, what you enter stays on your phone, and we never sell your information or use it for advertising.

What stays on your phone

Your due date, name (if you enter one), preferences, favorites, birth plan, hospital bag list, contraction history and downloaded meditations are stored only on your device. We don’t receive them. Deleting the app removes them.

Purchases

BirthWell Premium is a one-time purchase made through Apple. Apple handles payment; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat, a purchase-management service, to confirm whether your device owns Premium. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous customer ID created by the app, along with purchase and app-version details from Apple. It does not receive your name, email or the content you enter in BirthWell. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Meditation downloads

Meditations are downloaded from our storage provider, Supabase. Like any web server, it briefly records technical details such as your IP address and the file requested, for security and reliability. For Premium meditations, the app sends the anonymous RevenueCat ID so we can confirm your purchase. See Supabase’s privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow them, the morning word is scheduled on your phone. No notification content is sent to us.

Sharing

When you share or print your birth plan or bag list, you choose where it goes, using your phone’s own sharing options. We don’t receive a copy.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts, logins or email collection.
  • No advertising, ad tracking or selling of data.
  • No health records: BirthWell doesn’t connect to Apple Health or collect medical information.

Children

BirthWell is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can change your preferences, remove downloads, or turn off notifications at any time in the app or in your iPhone’s Settings. Because we don’t hold your personal content, deleting the app deletes your data. For questions about purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact us and we’ll help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above.
